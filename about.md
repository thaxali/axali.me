# Dr. Ax Ali — Creative Scientist & Entrepreneur

Dr. Abdullah "Ax" Ali (goes by Ax Ali) is a creative scientist, human-computer interaction (HCI) researcher, and AI product builder based in New York City. His career spans research science, design, engineering, product leadership, and entrepreneurship, with a throughline of working on 0-to-1 applied AI products.

He is the founder and CEO of **Seena Labs**, teaches and advises through **Studio89 Design**, and writes the newsletter **Everything is Designed**.

## What "Creative Scientist" means

Ax Ali calls himself a Creative Scientist because he has worn many hats: founder, product manager, designer, developer, and research scientist. He can bridge every seat at the product table — taking ideas from research insight to design, code, and market-ready products used by hundreds of thousands of people. This lets him accelerate product cycles, uncover opportunities, and raise the bar for user experience.

## Current work

- **Seena Labs (Founder & CEO):** A human-centered applied AI lab building products for understanding human-AI behavior and helping businesses build better products. Its first product is an AI Ambient Ethnographer — an always-on, AI-powered UX researcher embedded in a product with a single line of code. It observes the user's journey and interviews users during moments of interest, providing qualitative explanations for the quantitative behavior seen in usage analytics.
- **Studio89 Design:** Ax Ali's consulting and teaching studio, where he helps companies design and build applied AI products, workflows, and human-centered product strategies.
- **Adobe:** Works with Adobe as an AI and creativity expert for enterprise clients, giving workshops on using AI tools in creative workflows.

## Career history

- **3rd Brain (Founder & CEO):** Built a consumer AI organizer for couples — a mobile app and capture system that turned scattered thoughts, notes, and tasks into a structured shared "external brain." Grew to 500+ registered users; shipped iOS and Android apps.
- **Bilt Rewards (Technical Product Leader):** Led the loyalty products pod, shipping consumer rewards experiences and partner integrations connecting rent, travel, dining, payments, and loyalty. Work included Bilt Dining, Bilt Wallet card-linking, the Bilt x Lyft integration (89,000 account linkages in 24 hours), and the Bilt x point.me flight-search portal (~50,000 monthly searches).
- **Amazon (Product):** Led a low-price grocery bundling program across 12 markets ($55M incremental sales, $120M annual shipping savings) and customer-facing bundle tracker / promo surfaces. Led a cross-org "Working Backwards" + Design Thinking innovation workshop to reimagine Amazon grocery.
- **AWS (Product Innovation):** Prototyped and designed new products with enterprise clients across finance, healthcare, and industrial systems (clients including Itaú, Johnson & Johnson, ABB).
- **Apple:** Early multimodal interaction research and prototyping for what became Apple Vision Pro — a text-input system combining voice, gaze, and mid-air gestures.
- **Microsoft Research:** Accessibility and dyslexia-focused search research with the Ability group.
- **Carnegie Mellon University:** One of four scientists who co-invented Dytective, a game that uses AI to detect dyslexia.

## Research

Ax Ali's Ph.D. thesis direction was **Distributed Interaction Design**: using the web, machine learning, and human computation to run elicitation, co-design, and sensemaking research with people across the world. He has 20+ peer-reviewed publications across HCI, accessibility, and adaptive interfaces.

Selected work:
- **Dytective** — an AI game that detects dyslexia from play; has helped 500,000+ children via Change Dyslexia. (ASSETS Best Demo Award.)
- **Crowdlicit** — a two-sided system for running distributed end-user elicitation and design studies. (CHI 2019.)
- **Crowdsensus** — an ML-assisted workflow for large-scale qualitative data analysis. (UIST 2018.)
- Accessible search for people with dyslexia (Microsoft Research; CHI 2018, SIGIR 2017).
- Adaptive interfaces for variable pointing performance (UMBC; W4A, ASSETS).
